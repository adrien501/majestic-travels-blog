December in New York is crowded, expensive, cold, and completely unreasonable.

It is also magic when you plan it with enough space to breathe.

## Day one: arrive gently

Do not schedule your most important activity on arrival day. Walk, eat something warm, and let the city become real before you ask too much of it.

## Day two: classic lights

Rockefeller Center is busy for a reason. Go early, leave before your patience disappears, and use the rest of the day for quieter streets.

## The rhythm

Each day works best with three pieces:

- One iconic stop
- One neighborhood wander
- One warm indoor reset

That rhythm keeps the trip festive without turning it into a holiday obstacle course.
