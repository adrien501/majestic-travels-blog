Most travel quizzes ask where you want to go. A better question is how you want to feel when you get there.

## Start here

Choose the sentence that sounds most like you right now:

- I want quiet and a room with a window.
- I want a city that wakes me up.
- I want nature to make my problems feel smaller.
- I want to learn something with my hands.

## What it means

Your answer is not a personality type. It is a weather report.

Travel works better when you plan around your actual energy, not your fantasy self.
