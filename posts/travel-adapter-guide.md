Adapters are boring until you need one.

Then they become the difference between a charged phone and a small personal crisis beside an airport wall outlet.

## The simple packing rule

Pack one universal adapter and one spare charging cable. If you work while traveling, add a compact power strip or multi-port charger.

## Before you leave

Check three things:

- Plug type for your destination
- Voltage support on your charger
- Whether your accommodation has enough outlets

This is not glamorous travel advice. It is the kind that saves the first evening.
