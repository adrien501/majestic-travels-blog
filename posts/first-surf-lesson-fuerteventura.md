Before my first surf lesson, I spent an unreasonable amount of energy trying to look casual.

This is funny because nothing about carrying a beginner board says casual. It says: I am about to be humbled by water.

## What helped

The instructor did not make courage feel dramatic. That helped. The lesson was practical, kind, and repetitive in the best way.

- Put the board here
- Wait for the wave
- Keep your eyes forward
- Try again

## The good part

The good part was not standing up perfectly. The good part was realizing everyone was too busy with their own balance to judge mine.

That is a useful travel lesson too.
